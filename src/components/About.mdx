# About Me

Hello, My name is Badri. I go by bedro in online circles.

I am originally Georgian, but I live in the US.

I build backend systems — the parts of software that have to keep running after you ship.

My day job is at Leveldesk, a small managed services provider where I maintain IT infrastructure for 20+ SMB clients. Working as an operator has shaped how I write code: I reach for observable, maintainable solutions over clever ones, because I've debugged both at 2am.

Outside of work, I take on freelance projects and build things I wanted to exist. I stream the development process on Twitch — partly to share, partly because explaining your reasoning out loud is a fast way to find the holes in it.

I'm looking for developer roles where infrastructure and reliability actually matter. If that's you, reach out.
